Away from the screen

I draw. Mostly pencil, sometimes other things, and none of it has a deadline or a deploy script attached to it.

Pencil and other work

Ongoing

Drawing is the one thing I do where nothing is undoable. A line goes down and it either works or the page is spent, which is the opposite of everything else on this site, where the whole discipline is making mistakes cheap to reverse.

It is not unrelated to the paid work, though I would not push the point too far. Three dimensional modelling, page layout and colour work all reward the same habit of looking at a thing properly before touching it, and I got that habit from a pencil rather than from software.
