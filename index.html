A fee ledger that reconciles itself. A classroom of teachers who have never opened a spreadsheet. Both are the same job.

Most of what I build has money or records passing through it, which means it cannot be almost right. Kenya Product Index ranks brands on a weighted model rather than an average of stars.

The other half of the week is a classroom. I head training at the Siaya Community Digital Hub, where I have taken 1,576 people through digital skills, from children learning their first Scratch block to teachers across nine sub-counties. Teaching is what stops me from building things only I can operate.

I started in Turkana, fixing things that had to work without ideal conditions.

My first job in tech was as an ICT field officer in Lodwar. Turkana teaches you quickly that a system is only as good as the power, the connection and the person in front of it. I have built everything since with that in mind, which is why the work tends to include the parts nobody credits: the server in the corner, the DNS filtering, the tunnel that gets a machine past CGNAT.
